Why Spar

Reminders tell you it's time. Spar tells you what to do, shows you how, and remembers what you did.

Rounds, not guilt

Work hours, one call every interval: “Round 2 — calf raises ×20”. Three rounds a day rotated from eight moves. Small enough to actually happen between meetings.

Lives in your menu bar

One glanceable icon: orange when a round is waiting, green when today is done, gray off duty. Click the notification and the panel drops right under the tray.

Animated form demos

Every move carries a looping stick-figure demo with coaching cues — keyframed, not video, a few KB each. You always know what “good form” looks like.

Streak & heatmap

A GitHub-style contribution calendar, but for movement. Watch the greens add up, keep the 🔥 alive, and log bonus sets whenever you feel like it.

Local-first privacy

SQLite on your machine, zero telemetry, zero cloud. The camera counting in M2 runs fully on-device too — the leaderboard (M3) only ever sees the numbers you upload.

Seriously light

Tauri 2 with the system WebView — no Electron. Hand-written JS, no frameworks, no bundler. The tray icon is even drawn in code, pixel by pixel.

A round, in four beats

Set your work hours and interval once. Then:

1

It waits

Spar sits in the menu bar, off duty outside your work hours.

2

It calls

A notification names the round and the move. Snooze 10 minutes if you're mid-flow.

3

You spar

Follow the animated demo. Fifteen squats later, you're back — no one even noticed.

4

It remembers

Log the count, tick the round, and the heatmap turns another shade of green.
